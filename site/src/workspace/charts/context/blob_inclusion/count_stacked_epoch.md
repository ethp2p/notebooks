Each bar represents one epoch (32 slots); the stacked segments show how many slots in that epoch carried 0, 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, or 7-9 blobs.
A tall top segment (7-9) in consecutive epochs indicates periods of high demand, while a large bottom segment (0) signals missed or empty blocks.
Gaps or short bars may correspond to chain reorganizations or attestation deadlines missed during high-load periods.

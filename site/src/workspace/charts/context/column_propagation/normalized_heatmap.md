Per-column min-max normalization of median latency: 0 is the column's fastest bucket, 1 is its slowest.
Normalization makes all columns directly comparable regardless of their absolute latency baseline.
A column with a uniform pale band had little variation throughout the day; a column ranging from dark to bright had large intraday swings.
Columns whose custodians are geographically concentrated will tend to show correlated patterns with neighboring columns in the same custody set.
